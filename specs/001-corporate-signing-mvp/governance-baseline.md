# openSig Planning Constitution

Version: 0.1.0 draft; 2026-11-08. This records existing owner/repository constraints for planning and does not claim independent ratification.

## Principles

1. Original work Apache-2.0; shipped third-party materials MIT/Apache allowlist, notices and SBOM; no silent exceptions.
2. Human personal approval cannot be delegated to Hermes service credentials; preserve exact-version consent evidence.
3. Verify before completion; separate certificate integrity, trust, human authority and optional ledger proof.
4. Synthetic data only during development; isolate tenant access and keep secrets/PHI out of Git/logs.
5. Canonical governance/specification in spec leg, implementation in code leg, assembly exact pins; no implicit merges, production cutover or legal execution.

## Governance

OpenSpec governs proposed behavior; Speckit is the only executable task list. Independent acceptance evidence precedes release. Archive after matching leg and assembly landing. Proposed technical choices remain subject to qualification gates.
